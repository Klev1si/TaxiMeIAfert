4. Booking a ride

4.1 For passengers

  • You request a ride by entering pickup and destination addresses.
  • The app dispatches your request to the nearest available, approved driver matching your vehicle type.
  • You may add up to 5 intermediate stops or book a ride for a future time (scheduled at least 10 minutes ahead).
  • You can preview an estimated fare before submitting; the final fare is computed from the actual distance and duration of the trip and may differ from the estimate.
  • You can request a ride from a specific "saved driver" if you have previously rated them and they are currently online.

4.2 Driver acceptance

  • A driver may accept or decline a ride request within the time window shown on their screen.
  • If the assigned driver declines or does not respond, the request is automatically sent to the next-nearest driver.
  • The passenger may cancel a ride that has not yet been accepted at no charge.

5. Fares and pricing

5.1 How fares are calculated

Fares are calculated using an active tariff at the time the ride is accepted. A tariff consists of:

  • A base fare (charged regardless of distance)
  • A per-kilometre rate
  • A per-minute rate
  • A minimum fare (floor charged for very short trips)
  • Optionally, a night tariff active during a specified hour window
  • Optionally, a surge multiplier during periods of high demand

5.2 Who sets the tariff

  • Solo drivers set their own tariff via the "My Tariff" screen in the driver app.
  • Company drivers use the tariff set by their company; the active tariff is shown on the driver's home screen at all times so they know which rate applies.
  • Solo drivers without a personal tariff fall back to the platform's default tariff set by the administrator.

5.3 Cancellation fees

A cancellation fee may apply if a passenger cancels after a driver has been dispatched and is en route. The exact amount and conditions are displayed in the app before the passenger confirms a cancellation.

5.4 No-show fees

If a driver arrives at the pickup location, waits the required minimum time, and the passenger does not appear, the driver may report a no-show. The passenger may be charged a no-show fee in line with platform rules.

6. Payment

6.1 Cash payments

At launch, all rides are paid in cash directly to the driver at the end of the trip. Drivers should carry change. Passengers should pay the exact fare shown in the app.

6.2 Future card payments

Card payments through a regulated payment processor are planned for a future version of the app. When that becomes available, these Terms will be updated to describe the payment flow, refund process, and fees.

7. Commission structure (drivers and companies)

7.1 Solo drivers

Solo drivers keep 100% of the cash fare. When card payments are enabled in the future, solo drivers will keep approximately 90%, with a 10% platform fee deducted from the card share to cover payment processing and platform costs.

7.2 Company drivers

Company drivers keep a percentage of each fare set by their company (e.g. 70% to the driver, 30% to the company). The exact percentage is visible to the driver on the driver app's wallet screen. Companies may set a per-driver custom commission for individual drivers.

7.3 Settlements

Cash collected by a driver belongs to the driver (or, for company drivers, must be partially handed over to the company per the agreed commission). The app provides a Finances screen for companies to record settlements with drivers and a wallet for drivers to see what they have collected and what is owed.
